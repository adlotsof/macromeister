## Full description (4000 chars max)

MacroTrack is a focused calorie and macro tracker with an adaptive coaching
program — the numbers that matter, without the clutter.

TRACK WITHOUT THE BUSYWORK
• Log meals by quick-add, full-text search or barcode scan
• "AI describe": type what you ate ("bowl of pasta with olive oil") and get an
  instant macro estimate you can adjust
• Breakfast, lunch, dinner and snack sections with per-meal totals
• Copy yesterday's meals, log "fasted" zero-calorie days, quick-add calories
  only when you don't know the details
• Water tracking with a daily goal

A PROGRAM THAT ADAPTS TO YOU
• Set your goal: lose, maintain or gain weight, at your own weekly rate
• Your calorie and macro targets are computed from an adaptive energy-
  expenditure model, not a one-off formula
• Weekly check-in: confirm your weight and the coach adjusts tomorrow's
  targets based on your real trend — not wishful math
• Get clear advice when your intake and your weight trend diverge

SEE THE TREND, NOT JUST TODAY
• Weight trend chart with a fitted line that smooths daily noise
• 28-day intake history as bars against your target
• Photo timeline for visual progress
• Micronutrient tracking for 18 vitamins and minerals with editable targets

MAKE IT YOURS
• Dashboard cards you can show or hide
• Beautiful dark and light themes that follow the system
• Home-screen widget with your remaining calories, macros and water
• Daily and weekly check-in reminders, on your schedule

OFFLINE-FIRST, PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• The entire food database is bundled — search works with no account, no
  network, no tracking
• Automatic daily backups with rotation, plus manual CSV export and full JSON
  backup/restore via the share sheet
• Optional sync of weight, steps, nutrition and hydration with Google Health
  Connect / Apple Health
• Your data stays on your device. No account required.

MacroTrack is an independent project inspired by the workflows of adaptive
macro-coaching programs. It is not affiliated with MacroFactor.

Download MacroTrack and let the numbers do the coaching.
