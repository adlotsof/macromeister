Privacy Policy

MacroMeister (formerly MacroTrack) — Android & iOS
Effective date: 21 November 2026

Short version: MacroMeister has no accounts, no servers and no analytics. Your food log, weights, photos and settings live only on your device. We cannot see your data because it never reaches us.

1. Who we are

MacroMeister is an independent offline-first calorie and macro tracking app published by Tim Sonnenberg (Matten b. Interlaken, Switzerland — trsonn@gmail.com). This policy applies to both the Android (Google Play) and iOS (App Store) versions.

2. Data stored on your device (never sent to us)

  • Food log entries, custom foods, recipes and micronutrient data
  • Weight entries, program goals and check-in history
  • Progress photos (stored in the app's private storage)
  • Water intake, fasting timer history and app settings

This data stays in the app's local storage. There is no developer-operated backend, no telemetry, no crash reporting and no advertising SDKs.

3. Backups and exports

Automatic daily backups (rotated) and manual exports are written to your device storage and shared only when you use the share sheet (e.g. to save a copy in your own cloud drive). We never receive copies.

4. Third-party services (only when you use the feature)

  • Food search (Open Food Facts). When you search packaged products, the app sends your search term to the public Open Food Facts API (openfoodfacts.org) to retrieve product data. Search terms are not linked to you or your device identity by the app. Open Food Facts has its own privacy policy.
  • "AI describe" estimates. When you use the AI meal estimator, the meal description text you type (or the extracted label photo content) is sent to the AI provider you configured in the app's settings under your own API key (for example OpenRouter or OpenAI). The developer does not operate, proxy or log these requests; they are governed by your chosen provider's privacy policy.
  • Health platforms (optional, off by default). If you enable Health Connect (Android) or Apple Health (iOS) sync, weight, steps, nutrition and hydration data are exchanged directly with that platform on your device. It is never sent anywhere else.

5. Permissions

  • Camera — barcode scanning and photographing nutrition labels for the AI estimator. Camera images are processed for the requested log entry only and are not stored unless you save a progress photo.
  • Photo library — picking a label photo and saving progress photos you explicitly share.
  • Notifications — only the daily logging reminder and weekly check-in reminder you enable.
  • Health Connect / Apple Health — only if you turn the sync on in Settings.

6. Children

MacroMeister is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone, of any age.

7. Your rights

Because all personal data resides on your device, you have full control: use CSV/JSON export to take a copy, or uninstall the app to erase everything it stores. For questions or deletion requests concerning anything else, contact trsonn@gmail.com.

8. Changes to this policy

Substantial changes will be reflected in the app's release notes and on this page with a new effective date.
